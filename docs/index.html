zet

A terminal multiplexer for Windows, written in Rust, with its own VT engine and a GPU renderer.

Status — pre-alpha

zet builds and runs: it opens a window, starts a shell, and draws it with its own VT engine and its own GPU renderer. It speaks the kitty keyboard protocol in both directions — the flag stack, the query, and the CSI u encoding, with Report alternate keys the one flag it does not implement. What is missing is the depth around that: the update check exists as zet --check-update and zet --update, and never runs on launch — nothing is planned to change that. It installs and runs as a daily driver for the person who wrote it, and pre-alpha is the honest label for what that is worth to anyone else.
